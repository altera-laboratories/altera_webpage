Color · Paper surfaces

The cream the page is printed on.

Five warm paper tones carry every surface in the Reading Room, from the page base up to card stock, dividers, and borders. Stack them on the cream and the hierarchy reads like an academic press: light, layered, intentional.

--paper-50 #faf5ea

Lifted surface. The lightest stock, used for swatch faces, inset wells, and panels that need to float above the page.

--paper-100 #f3ead8

Page base. The cream the whole site is printed on. This is the body background everything else sits on.

--paper-200 #ebdec5

Card stock. The default fill for cards and notecards, one warm step above the page so blocks read as raised paper.

--paper-300 #ddccac

Divider. Hairline rules and section seams, dark enough to separate without breaking the warm field.

--paper-400 #c9b793

Border. The strongest paper tone, reserved for card outlines and the edges that frame a collage tile.
