Foundations · Elevation

Shadows.

Three steps of warm elevation, lifted off cream paper rather than black glass. Each shadow is tinted with ink, not pure neutral, so a raised card still reads as paper on a desk. Focus stays royal blue, reserved for the moment a control takes input.

Step 01

Resting paper.

A concept tile barely off the surface. Used for inline cards and list rows.

Step 02

Lifted card.

The default for content cards and the mastery panels in the Reading Room.

Step 03

Pinned notecard.

Tacked above the board for callouts and a single re-teach flag. Hover to settle it.

--shadow-pin

Focus ring.

Tab to either control. The ring is a soft royal-blue halo, the one place blue earns its keep on paper, paired with the resting paper shadow so focus reads as lift, not alarm.
